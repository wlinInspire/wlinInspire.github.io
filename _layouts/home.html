Wei Lin

Staff Software Engineer · Distributed Systems & Cloud-Native Infrastructure · Los Angeles, CA

Go Python C/C++ Distributed Systems AWS (Lambda / EKS / Kinesis) WebRTC & Real-Time Streaming ML Deployment

Wei is a Staff Software Engineer with 10+ years building 0-to-1 platforms, most recently distributed access-control infrastructure and real-time streaming systems on AWS. He has been part of two startups that reached successful acquisitions: Openpath Security (acquired by Motorola Solutions) and Inspire Energy (acquired by Shell).

He holds a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and researches optimization, game theory, and multi-agent systems, and lives in Los Angeles, CA.
